EHR Risk Framework

Alternatives to opaque clinical AutoML

If you are searching for alternatives to black-box clinical AutoML for research and education, prefer tools that expose splits, leakage checks, calibration, and explanations—not only a leaderboard AUROC.

This page does not claim clinical superiority. Scope is methods teaching and reproducible research software.

What to look for

  • Explicit index time / prediction horizon
  • Temporal or group CV—not random row CV on longitudinal EHR
  • Calibration metrics and plots
  • Inspectable models + SHAP (or similar)
  • Clear “not a medical device” framing

EHR Risk Framework as an open alternative

An open-source framework for leakage-safe, calibrated, and explainable EHR risk prediction— Docker workbench, FastAPI jobs, light research HPO (not clinical AutoML), and downloadable reports.
