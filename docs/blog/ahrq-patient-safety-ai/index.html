AHRQ digital healthcare research and the case for evidence-based AI methods

AHRQ’s Digital Healthcare Research Program focuses on whether digital tools—including AI—improve care safely and equitably. Methods quality is part of that evidence story.

For research and education only. Outputs are not clinical recommendations and are not intended for patient care. We are working toward broader general-purpose use in the future. Posts summarize public sources for methods teaching—verify primary literature before citing.

AHRQ’s digital healthcare focus

AHRQ’s Digital Healthcare Research Program supports research on digital tools that affect delivery, safety, and value. Program portal: digital.ahrq.gov.

Funding announcements have solicited work on how AI systems affect patient safety and how they can be implemented safely— emphasizing evidence, not hype.

How the EHR Risk Framework helps

For investigators and students studying predictive modeling methods (not bedside deployment), the workbench provides leakage audits, calibration reporting, and explainability jobs so “safety-relevant” evaluation dimensions are practiced before anyone claims readiness.
