Limitations & model card

What this framework does not claim. Use alongside the capability pages when writing theses or lab reports.

Research and education only. Not a regulated medical device. Not for patient care.

Clinical / legal / safety

  • Not for diagnosis, treatment, or triage without institutional governance
  • No FDA/CE claim — outputs are research probabilities only
  • You own PHI, IRB, DUA, and PhysioNet credentialing
  • Local API is unsafe on public networks without API_KEY

Modeling boundaries

Supported

Logistic regression, random forest, XGBoost, optional LightGBM. Windowed tabular features. Optional isotonic calibration. SHAP explanations.

Not supported

LSTM / Transformers / foundation models as a production path. Full FHIR/OMOP servers. Multi-tenant SaaS. Full AutoML HPO.

Data & science honesty

  • Demo CSVs are for smoke tests — not clinical performance claims
  • MIMIC-IV requires your PhysioNet credentials; restricted data is never shipped
  • Leakage audit catches common mistakes — not every contamination pattern
  • SHAP explains the fitted model — not causal effects
  • Fairness helpers need group columns; otherwise they skip with a reason
  • Calibration improves probability quality; it does not guarantee clinical utility

Platform

  • Single-host research workbench — one heavy job at a time
  • Filesystem experiment runs — not a full MLflow/W&B replacement
  • Upload size default ~50MB; SQL import is read-only SELECT
