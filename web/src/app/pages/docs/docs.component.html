Intended use

For research and education only. Outputs are not clinical recommendations and are not intended for patient care. We are working toward broader general-purpose use in the future. It is not FDA-cleared or CE-marked, is not a medical device, and must not guide diagnosis or treatment without institutional validation and qualified human oversight.
